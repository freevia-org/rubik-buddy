Solve your standard 3x3 cube with camera scanning and animated, step-by-step moves.

SCAN SIX FACES
Hold the cube as shown and scan each face in turn. The app guides you through the rotations so the faces line up correctly.

CHECK THE COLORS
Lighting can make similar colors look alike. Check each scan and tap a sticker to correct its color before solving. Rubik Buddy checks the cube state and reports impossible scans.

FOLLOW EACH MOVE
Watch the solution on a draggable 3D cube. Step forward, go back, or replay a move at your own pace.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
Camera frames are processed on your device and discarded after analysis. No photos or videos are saved or uploaded. No account, ads, analytics, or tracking. Scanning and solving work offline.

Designed for a standard 3x3 cube with white, yellow, red, orange, blue, and green stickers. Use good, even lighting for the best scan results. A physical cube and camera access are needed for scanning.
