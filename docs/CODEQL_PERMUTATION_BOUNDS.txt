the multiplication. The uint64_t right operand subsequently promotes the

Induction bounds every actual idx and multiplication by B_i and P_i.

multiplication nor the subsequent conversion to int32_t overflows for
any reachable n. This is an arithmetic proof, not a claim that every
malformed array has defined shifts or that the entire solver is safe.
